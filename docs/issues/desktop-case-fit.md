# desktop case fit

the november 9 plan compared the lancool 207 and d41 mesh. the dimensions
leave little room around the kraken x62, the z390 aorus ultra heatsinks and
the tall dominator ram. the e650 power leads also need room to bend. a failed
fit could require returning the case.

the 207 permits a 60 mm top radiator assembly. the kraken with fans is about
56 mm thick. the d41 manual specifies top radiator and conditional gpu
clearances. a dry-fit establishes the clearance on the actual board.
[case and assembly](../desktop.md#case-and-assembly).

close after recording the chosen case and layout, fitting the cooler, board,
ram and original psu cables, and closing both panels without contact or
forced bends. check drive positions before allocating bottom fan mounts.
