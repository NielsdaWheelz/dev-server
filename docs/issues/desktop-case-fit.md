# desktop case fit

the d41 layout puts the kraken x62 at the roof
and the e650 in the highest front bracket position. the kraken, z390 aorus
ultra heatsinks, tall dominator ram and original e650 leads need a physical
clearance check. [assembly walkthrough](../jonsbo-d41-walkthrough.html).

the kraken with fans is about 56 mm thick. the d41 manual lists top
radiator support and conditional graphics card clearances. dry-fit the
stack with the board and memory installed and the processor power cable
routed before applying paste.
[case and assembly](../desktop.md#case-and-assembly).

close after recording the installed layout and closing both panels without
contact or forced bends. check all three bottom fan mounts are clear of the
drive cage and its fasteners, and check rear fan clearance with the radiator
in place.
